# LAB654 lab manual

**Copilot Connectors: Connect once and bring your enterprise knowledge to every workflow**

Welcome! In this hands-on lab you'll connect trusted data sources to Microsoft 365 Copilot, then use connectors, skills and write actions to finish real workflows across finance, legal and productivity.

## How this lab works

1. Read the [Introduction](01-introduction.md) and complete [Getting started with connectors](02-getting-started.md).
2. **Pick two of the three tracks.** If you finish early, try the third on your own.
3. Wrap up with the [Summary](06-summary.md).

## Modules

| # | Module | What you'll do | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Introduction](01-introduction.md) | Meet Marcus at Verdantis; learn the synced, federated and hybrid connector models | ~5 min |
| 2 | [Getting started with connectors](02-getting-started.md) | Find, connect and try your connectors | ~10 min |
| 3 | [Finance track](03-finance.md) | Build an NVIDIA investment committee pack with LSEG, FactSet and Moody's | ~18 min |
| 4 | [Legal track](04-legal.md) | Review and redline an AI vendor contract with Work IQ, Harvey and iManage | ~15 min |
| 5 | [Productivity track](05-productivity.md) | Fix a slipped launch plan across Smartsheet, Notion and the intranet | ~10 min (+3) |
| 6 | [Summary](06-summary.md) | Recap and next steps | ~2 min |

## Before you begin

- [ ] You have your **seat card** with your lab sign-in details.
- [ ] You can sign in to Microsoft 365 Copilot in the lab browser.
- [ ] You have this manual open beside Copilot.

> [!NOTE]
> Everything runs in a demo tenant with fictional people, companies and data. Use only the credentials on your own seat card.
