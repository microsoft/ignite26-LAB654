# LAB654 lab manual

**Copilot Connectors: Connect once and bring your enterprise knowledge to every workflow**

Welcome! In this hands-on lab you'll connect trusted data sources to Microsoft 365 Copilot, then use connectors, skills and write actions to finish real workflows. Pick the track closest to your work, then bring your own app to Copilot.

## How this lab works

1. Read the [Introduction](01-introduction.md) and complete [Getting started with connectors](02-getting-started.md).
2. **Pick one of the four tracks.** If you finish early, try another on your own.
3. Everyone completes [Bring your own app](07-custom-mcp.md).
4. Wrap up with the [Summary](08-summary.md).

## Modules

| # | Module | What you'll do | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Introduction](01-introduction.md) | Meet Emil at Zava and the connector catalog | ~5 min |
| 2 | [Getting started with connectors](02-getting-started.md) | Find, connect and try your connectors | ~10 min |
| 3 | [Finance track](03-finance.md) | Build an NVIDIA investment committee pack with LSEG, FactSet and Moody's | ~18 min |
| 4 | [Legal track](04-legal.md) | Flag, redline and prepare to negotiate an AI vendor contract with iManage | ~15 min |
| 5 | [Productivity track](05-productivity.md) | Prep a sales pitch with Test_Salesforce | ~10 min |
| 6 | [Engineering track](06-engineering.md) | Unblock a customer bug fix with Test_Jira and Test_GitHub | ~15 min |
| 7 | [Bring your own app](07-custom-mcp.md) | Connect an in-house expense app through MCP and file a trip | ~20 min |
| 8 | [Summary](08-summary.md) | Recap, governance and next steps | ~3 min |
