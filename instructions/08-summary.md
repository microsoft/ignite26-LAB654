## Key takeaways

1. **Connect once, use everywhere.** A connector set up once works across the Copilot experiences that support it: Copilot Chat, Cowork, Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
2. **The catalog covers the popular tools; MCP covers the rest.** Pick from 300+ connectors, and connect your own apps with no code.
3. **Skills bring the method, not just the data.** Partner skills shape how Copilot works with each source, from call prep to sprint slides.
4. **Write actions stay in your control.** Copilot acts with your permissions, and only after you approve.
5. **From answers to finished work.** You can find the information, decide and act without leaving Copilot.

## Govern what you connect

Every connector you used today, including your own app, is managed in the same place:

- **Admins decide what's available.** Allow or block connectors and org-built apps in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
- **Roll out gradually.** Use staged rollout to start with a pilot group.
- **Approvals before writes.** Copilot asks before it sends, books, updates or submits anything.

## Your next step

Enable the connectors that matter most to your organization. Start with the systems where your people spend their day, such as your CRM, ticketing, code, documents and line-of-business apps, so Copilot can help them finish the work, not just find it.
