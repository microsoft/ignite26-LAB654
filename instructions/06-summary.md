# Summary

## What you did

| Track | Read | Decide | Act | Communicate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finance | Earnings vs consensus (LSEG), peer profitability (FactSet), credit (Moody's) | Scenarios and a pressure-tested view | Built the NVIDIA Committee Pack in Excel | Committee memo in Word |
| Legal | Decisions from email and Teams; the law from Harvey | Gaps against the contract and playbook (iManage) | Redline with comments in Word | Negotiation pack and emails through Cowork |
| Productivity | A scheduled catch-up across Notion and email | Fix checked against the intranet playbook | Updated the tracker and decision log in Notion | Partner email and an approval meeting |

## Key takeaways

1. **Connect once, use everywhere.** A connector set up once works across the Copilot experiences that support it: Copilot Chat, Cowork, Word and Excel.
2. **Two connector models, one answer.** Synced and federated connectors work together in a single prompt, each with citations.
3. **Skills bring the method, not just the data.** Partner skills, such as the LSEG and Moody's analyst skills, shape how Copilot works with the data.
4. **Write actions stay in your control.** Copilot acts with your permissions, and only after you approve.
5. **Read → decide → act → communicate.** You can finish the whole loop without leaving Copilot.
