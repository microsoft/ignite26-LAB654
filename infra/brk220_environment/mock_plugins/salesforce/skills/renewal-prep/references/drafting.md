# Drafting rules

## Proposal email

Subject: `<Account> FY<yy> renewal proposal`

Body, in this order, under 200 words, plain and direct:

1. One line thanking the buyer and referring to the agreement in their email (date and position, for example "as agreed on October 8").
2. **Pricing:** the renewal fee with the agreed uplift, as a percentage and an amount, against the current fee and the contract maximum. For example: "Your subscription renews at $2,520,000 a year, a 5% uplift, below the 7% your current order form allows."
3. **Price sensitivity:** one sentence acknowledging the budget pressure they raised (from the QBR), and what stays the same at no extra cost (scope, EU data residency, 1-hour P1 response).
4. **The open ask:** the roadmap commitment, in the Product roadmap's approved wording.
5. **Service:** that the open incidents now have a named owner (only if step 1 assigned them), by ticket key, and that the customer success manager will send updates.
6. Next step: the contract draft follows for review; a date to talk.

Don't mention the competitor by name, don't disparage it and don't offer anything that isn't in the agreed position. Sign with the user's name and title.

## Renewal order form

Fill every [bracketed] field of the template. Sources:

| Template field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Customer legal name, MSA date, prior order form number | As in the current order form | Current order form |
| Service, scope, regions | Carry over | Current order form |
| Renewal term | Day after the current term ends, for the renewal length in the renewal clause (12 months unless agreed otherwise) | Current order form, renewal clause |
| Annual subscription fee | Current fee × (1 + agreed uplift) | Salesforce amount, email |
| Change from prior term | For example "+5% (+$120,000), within the 7% renewal cap; uplift capped at 5% as agreed on October 8, 2026" | Email, current order form |
| Renewal, service levels, data residency and identity | Carry over unchanged | Current order form |
| Roadmap commitments | Committable items only, approved wording | Product roadmap page |
| Approvals | State whether director approval is needed: not needed when the fee equals the agreed position | Template rules |
| Signatures | Leave names blank; keep the titles of the prior signatories | Current order form |

Put "DRAFT – for Legal review" at the top. Keep the template's section numbering.
